authors = ["Martin Kunz"]

description = """
Generating random samples (otherwise known as variates), has become a part for many widely used algorithms, one example
of such being Monte Carlo integration, which can be used for Bayesian statistical inference. As a result, it has become
a necessity to be able to generate variates from a distribution in an efficient manner. In this post, I will show the 
principle behind two powerful procedures which can be used for generating such samples from a wide variety of 
distributions. A sampler for the very flexible generalized inverse Gaussian (GIG) distribution is then developed by the 
use of these procedures. To prove its worth, an example of Bayesian inference of the speed of vehicles on a highway 
using this sampler is presented.
"""
